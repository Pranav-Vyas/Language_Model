# Phase 3 — synthetic reasoning dataset statistics

Generated by `scripts/build_reasoning_stats.py` from the files that `<lang>/reasoning/generate.py` produces. Do not edit by hand.

The two datasets are **independent**: separate generators in separate trees, separate frozen tokenizers, separate weights, and templates written from each language's own corpus evidence rather than translated. The two entity pools are NOT name-disjoint and are not required to be — most South Asian given names are shared across both languages, and forcing disjointness is what previously pushed the Nepali pool into common nouns. Pool SIZE is held identical, because that would affect the H-vs-L comparison.

## Surface-heuristic baselines (train)

A guesser that always names the first- or last-mentioned entity must not beat chance, or a high accuracy would prove nothing. Premise order is shuffled, question polarity is balanced, and each premise is randomly surfaced with the adjective or its antonym.
